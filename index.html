What we build

Built in Stone. Designed Around You.

Five things we do more than anything else, all designed, built and guaranteed by one owner-operated company.

  1. 01
    Free consultation

    We walk the yard with you, look at grade and drainage, and give you straight advice, at no charge.

  2. 02
    Custom 2D & 3D design

    You see the patio, wall or fireplace in a digital design before anything gets dug, and change it until it's right.

  3. 03
    Built by our own crew

    The people who designed it build it, with the same attention to detail from the base course to the last capstone.

Patios & Outdoor Rooms

Paver or natural stone, with pergolas and arbors to finish the space. Patios are often the most used “room” in the house, so we design yours like one. Free patio designs and estimates.

Retaining Walls & Steps

Turn an unusable slope into level, usable ground. We build retaining, freestanding and seating walls, pillars, steps and stairways, set on a proper base to stand up to Minnesota freeze-thaw.

Fireplaces & Fire Pits

From full outdoor fireplaces with mantels to simple fire pits, wood or gas, big or small. They stretch a short Minnesota season into October. You just bring the marshmallows.

Paver Driveways

A driveway is the first thing anyone sees. We lay paver driveways on a properly compacted base, in a classic look or a custom pattern: durable, attractive and made to last.

Water Features

From backyard bubbling boulders to front-entry fountains, and the ponds, streams and creek beds in between. No landscape is complete without the sound of water.

Not Sure Where to Start?

Most projects are a combination: a wall that makes room for a patio, a patio that wants a fire pit. Tell us about the yard and we'll design it as a whole.
